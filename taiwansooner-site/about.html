About Us

Since 2000, Taiwan Sooner Industries Co. has been dedicated to designing and manufacturing a wide variety of headphones. Our product range includes wired and wireless headphones and earbuds, designed for gaming, Bluetooth, noise cancellation, industrial, and educational applications. We also manufacture speakers and microphones. In addition, we offer OEM and ODM services to clients worldwide.
